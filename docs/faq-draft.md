## 1. What should I wear to my first class? Do you lend gis?
**Draft:** Comfortable athletic clothes you can move in — a fitted t-shirt or rash guard and shorts or athletic pants without zippers or pockets. Remove jewelry and trim your nails. Bring a water bottle and sandals for walking off the mat. Let us know when you schedule your free class and we'll tell you what to expect.

## 2. What ages do you teach? Is there a minimum age?
**Draft:** Our youth classes (Youth Jiu Jitsu and Youth Nogi) are for kids ages 5 to 12. Students 13 and older train in our adult classes (Adult Gi and Adult Nogi). The minimum age is 5.

## 3. I've never trained or I'm out of shape. Can I still join?
**Draft:** Yes. Most of our students started with no experience. Every class welcomes beginners, and our coaches teach fundamentals from day one. You get in shape by training — you don't need to be in shape to start.

## 9. What's the difference between gi and nogi? *(added by web team)*
**Draft:** Gi classes train in the traditional jiu-jitsu uniform (kimono), where grips on the collar and sleeves shape the game. Nogi classes train in athletic clothing — rash guard and shorts — with a faster, grip-light style. Both teach the same core grappling, and many students train both.
